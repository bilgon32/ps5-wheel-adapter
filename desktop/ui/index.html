01

Build the adapter.

The Pico needs an added USB host port: D+ to GPIO0, D− to GPIO1, 5 V to VBUS, and ground to GND. Connect a USB hub to that port.

Use your wheel’s normal power supply. Connect its pedals and shifter to the G27.

02

Install & connect.

For first installation, hold BOOTSEL while plugging the Pico into the PC. Use Install & backup to flash the bundled image.

Then click Connect Pico. If it appears in console mode, choose Open setup mode. Firmware older than G27 Studio needs the BOOTSEL installation first.

03

Make it yours.

Select your authentication controller. Use the original G27 brake or choose an external pedal and its axis. Capture the released and pressed positions if needed.

Choose a force profile, import your measured LUT, and check your inputs on the live dashboard.

04

Connect to PS5.

Save & switch to console, then connect the Pico to PS5. Keep the wheel and licensed controller on its hub. The wheel should calibrate normally.

Press the two middle red shifter buttons together for the PS button. In GT7, the clutch needs manual transmission and a compatible car.

WHEN SOMETHING DOESN’T CLICK

A few helpful checks.

The Pico doesn’t appear.

Use a USB data cable. Hold BOOTSEL while reconnecting to install G27 Studio firmware. For regular setup, choose Connect Pico and open setup mode. Your wheel connects to the hub, not directly to the PC.

The wheel appears, but there are no inputs.

Check the G27’s power supply and wait for calibration. Keep its pedals and shifter connected. Try another USB hub if downstream devices do not appear.

The external brake points the wrong way.

Capture the released position with your foot off the pedal, then capture the pressed position. Alternatively, use Swap pedal direction. Test without saving before committing a calibration.

There is no force feedback in the dashboard.

A PC setup session does not receive GT7’s forces. Use the brief pulse to test the wheel path. Game force feedback resumes after switching to console mode and connecting to PS5.

The wheel connects to PS5, but authentication fails.

Use a compatible licensed PS4 specialty controller. Hori Wired Controller Light for PS4 is tested. A standard DualShock 4 cannot authenticate this adapter. Check the selected controller and its USB connection.
