G27 STUDIO — START HERE

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Open G27-Studio/G27-Studio.exe.
2. Connect your G27, licensed PS4 specialty controller, and optional load-cell
   pedal to the USB hub attached to your Pico. Power the G27 normally.
3. For the first installation, hold the Pico's BOOTSEL button while connecting
   its built-in USB port to your PC. In Install & backup, install the image.
4. Click Connect Pico. Open setup mode if the adapter is in console mode.
5. Select your controller and brake device in Devices & pedals. Press the pedal
   to identify its axis; capture released and pressed positions if needed.
6. Choose your force profile or import a .lut. Check the live dashboard.
7. Save & switch to console. Move the Pico's built-in cable to PS5.

The wheel, controller, and pedal stay on the Pico's downstream hub.
Press the two middle red shifter buttons together for the PS button.
Hold the two outer red shifter buttons for one second to change force profiles.

Windows 10/11 x64. If the app cannot open its window, install Microsoft Edge
WebView2 Runtime: https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
Python and a compiler are not needed for the packaged application.

Firmware and source: https://github.com/bilgon32/ps5-wheel-adapter
Downloads: https://github.com/bilgon32/ps5-wheel-adapter/releases
